Dear APL Publications Team,

Could you please forward this historical engineering-records inquiry to APL Archives or the appropriate Space Department records custodian? I understand this address handles publication requests; I would appreciate a referral if another office holds the project files.

I am reconstructing the science telemetry of Transit 5E-5 (1964-083C, NORAD 959), launched in December 1964. I seek the original flight telemetry format and encoder/interface drawings: the 16-bit PCM word and subframe schedule, the assignments and calibration of the three 35-channel PAM commutators, the eight command/telltale definitions, and any status-bit truth tables. Ground decommutator manuals, pass printouts with channel headings, and the 5E-5 data-processing program/listing or “Dictionary I/II” documentation would also be valuable. Please include revisions or mode documentation if available.

Potential search terms are Transit 5E-5, 5E-2, APL Space Department Telemetry and Command Group, spacecraft S/N 43, TG-890 (AD 652 807), TG-886 (AD 657 155), SDO-1600, and Navy contract NOw 62-0604-c. An earlier article by J. W. Hamblen and J. B. Oakes, “Instrumentation and Telemetry of Transit Navigational Satellites” (*Electronics*, 11 August 1961, pp. 148–153), names C. A. Blackburn and F. K. Preikschat as designers of a predecessor Transit telemetry encoder. Their engineering files or successor design drawings may offer another way to locate the 5E-5 format; I do not assume the Transit 1B/2-A formats apply to 5E-5. I already have the published reports TG-890 and SDO-1600; the missing material is the underlying word/bit/channel assignment. An NSSDCA catalog identifies the 5E-5 magnetometer data as 64-083C-01A / D000242 and describes processing on an IBM 7094.

My observed radio source tracks NORAD 965 / 1964-083D, which catalogs identify as the co-launched Transit 5B-5 / Oscar 2. I am checking the historical C/D designation ambiguity, so any documentation tying serial numbers and transmitters to those designations would help as well.
